How an exam runs

System check

Camera, microphone, full-screen and network are tested before the exam starts.

Identity + liveness

The student blinks and turns their head, then the start photo is matched against their registered face.

Monitored exam

The AI watches face, objects, audio and browser activity. Proctors see live tiles and receive alerts.

Review & report

Proctors review the timeline with evidence, confirm or dismiss events, record a verdict and export reports.
