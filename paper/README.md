# IEEE paper

- `ieee_paper.tex` – IEEE conference paper (IEEEtran). Upload to Overleaf or build with `pdflatex ieee_paper.tex` (run twice).
- `ieee_paper.pdf` – compiled paper.
- `results/` – figures and `results.json` produced by `python scripts/evaluate.py` (set `FACES_DIR` to a folder of single-face photos for the face and latency sections).

Before submitting: replace the author names, affiliations and e-mails (marked `TODO` in the .tex file).
